                          B L A C K J A C K   T R A I N E R

                            Instruction Manual  (v1)

==============================================================================

A pocket blackjack game that teaches you to play well. It runs entirely on your
device (no internet needed once installed), uses chips only — never real money —
and shows you the mathematically best move, a live card count, and a simulator
that reveals how betting systems really behave.

This manual is written for someone brand new to blackjack. If you already know
the game, skim sections 1, 4, and 6-10.

==============================================================================
  1.  ABOUT THIS GAME
==============================================================================

This is a *trainer*, not a casino. The goal is to learn optimal blackjack and to
understand betting and card-counting, with three teaching tools built in:

   * A basic-strategy ADVISOR that tells you the best move and why.
   * A live card COUNTER (Hi-Lo) as the cards come out.
   * A betting SIMULATOR that runs thousands of sessions and charts the results.

You play with chips. There is no real money and nothing to win or lose but
bragging rights. Your chip balance ("bankroll") is saved on your device between
sessions.


==============================================================================
  2.  QUICK START (YOUR FIRST HAND)
==============================================================================

   1. PLACE A BET. Tap the chip buttons at the bottom ($5, $25, $100, $500) to
      stack up a wager, then tap DEAL. (Tap CLEAR to start the bet over.)

   2. PLAY YOUR HAND. You and the dealer each get two cards; one of the dealer's
      cards stays face-down. Try to get closer to 21 than the dealer WITHOUT
      going over. Tap HIT to take a card or STAND to stop. (Double, Split, and
      Surrender are explained in section 5.)

   3. SEE THE RESULT. The dealer reveals the hidden card and plays out their
      hand. A badge shows whether you Won, Lost, Pushed (tied), or hit Blackjack.
      Tap NEXT HAND to play again.

Stuck on a decision? Tap "Show basic-strategy hint" during your turn and the game
will tell you the best play and explain it (see section 6).


==============================================================================
  3.  BLACKJACK BASICS
==============================================================================

THE GOAL
   Beat the dealer's hand without your total going over 21. Going over 21 is a
   "bust" and you lose immediately.

CARD VALUES
   * Number cards 2-10 are worth their number.
   * Jack, Queen, King are each worth 10.
   * An Ace is worth 11 OR 1 -- whichever helps you more.

SOFT vs HARD HANDS
   * A hand where an Ace is counted as 11 is "soft" (it can't bust on the next
     card, because the Ace can drop to 1). Example: Ace + 6 = "soft 17".
   * If there is no Ace, or the Ace must count as 1 to avoid busting, the hand is
     "hard". Example: 10 + 7 = "hard 17".
   The game labels your total as "· soft" when an Ace is still counting as 11.

BLACKJACK (a "natural")
   An Ace plus any 10-value card as your FIRST TWO cards = 21, called a
   "blackjack" or "natural". It beats any other 21 and normally pays extra (3:2 —
   see section 9). A 21 made from three or more cards, or after splitting, is just
   "21", not a blackjack.

PUSH
   A tie with the dealer. Your bet is returned; nobody wins.

HOW A ROUND PLAYS OUT
   You act first. If you bust, you lose right away (the dealer need not draw).
   If you stand, the dealer reveals the face-down card and draws until reaching at
   least 17, then stops. Closest to 21 wins; equal totals push.

THE COUNT READOUT (just under the top bar)
   The live Hi-Lo card count: Running count, True count, and how many decks are
   left in the shoe. Green means the count favors you, red means it favors the
   house. Full details in section 7. (You can turn this off in the config.)

THE TABLE
   DEALER row .. The dealer's cards and their total. During your turn one card is
                 face-down ([ ?? ]) and no total is shown yet.
   YOU row ..... Your hand(s): the cards, the total (e.g. "16", "soft 18", or

   On a dealer Ace .. an insurance prompt: "Insure $X" or "No" (section 5).

                      Surrender. A button is greyed out when that move isn't
                      legal right now.
   After the ROUND .. a single NEXT HAND button.

   * "Shuffling…" appears briefly when the shoe is reshuffled.
   * A "Config notes" panel appears only if the rules file asks for something this

==============================================================================
  5.  PLAYING A ROUND: THE ACTIONS
==============================================================================

A round flows through stages: Betting -> (Insurance, if offered) -> Your turn ->
Dealer's turn -> Result. Your options:

HIT
   Take another card. You can keep hitting until you stand or bust. Reaching 21
   ends your turn automatically.

STAND
   Stop with your current total and pass play to the next hand (or the dealer).

DOUBLE (down)
   Offered only on your first two cards. You DOUBLE your wager and take EXACTLY
   ONE more card, then your turn ends. Use it when one card is likely to win.
   (Greyed out if you can't cover the extra chips.)

SPLIT
   Offered when your first two cards are a matching pair (any two 10-value cards
   count, e.g. K + 10). It splits them into TWO separate hands, each getting a
   second card and a matching wager. You play them one at a time. You may split
   again up to a total of FOUR hands. Special case: split ACES get exactly ONE
   card each and then stand. (Greyed out if you can't cover the extra wager or
   you've reached the hand limit.)

DOUBLE AFTER SPLIT
   In this game you ARE allowed to double a hand that came from a split (often
   written "DAS").

SURRENDER (late surrender)
   Offered as your very first action on a fresh two-card hand. You give up and
   forfeit HALF your bet, ending the round. It's "late" surrender: it's offered
   only after the dealer has checked for blackjack. Use it on truly hopeless hands
   (the advisor will tell you which).

INSURANCE
   Offered the moment the dealer's up-card is an Ace, before you play. It's a side
   bet of half your wager that the dealer has blackjack. If they do, insurance
   pays 2:1 (you break even on the round); if they don't, you lose the side bet.
   Basic strategy says to decline it unless you're counting — tap "No".

A note on money: everything is chips. Your bet is taken when committed and paid
back at settlement. Win pays even money (1:1), blackjack pays 3:2, a push returns
your bet. If you can't even cover the table minimum, see section 12.


==============================================================================
  6.  THE BASIC-STRATEGY ADVISOR (THE HINT)
==============================================================================

During your turn, tap

      💡 Show basic-strategy hint

and the game shows the single best move, like:

      Basic strategy: Stand
      Standing (EV +0.15) beats hitting (-0.02) — the dealer's 6 busts about
      42% of the time, so let them draw.
      Computed from your rules — cross-check an authoritative chart for the
      closest calls.

If you then make a DIFFERENT move, a note flags it for that hand:

      Basic strategy here was Stand

HOW IT WORKS (and why you can trust it)
   "Basic strategy" is the move that loses the least (or wins the most) on
   average for a given hand vs. the dealer's up-card. This game does NOT look the
   answer up in a memorized chart. Instead it COMPUTES it from your exact rules:

      1. It builds the probability of each dealer final total from the up-card
         (respecting whether the dealer hits or stands on soft 17, and the peek).
      2. It works out the expected value (EV) of standing, hitting, doubling,
         splitting, and surrendering.
      3. It recommends the highest-EV legal move, and the explanation quotes those
         real numbers and the dealer's bust chance.

   Because it's computed, it automatically adapts if you change the rules (H17 vs
   S17, double-after-split, surrender, the double restriction, and so on).

   EV ("expected value") is measured in bets: +1.0 means you'd win one bet on
   average, -1.0 means lose one, 0 is break-even.

THE HONEST CAVEAT
   For a handful of razor-close decisions (the classic example is Ace,2 vs a
   dealer 5), the underlying math can land on the opposite side of some published
   charts by a hair. For those edge cases, cross-check an authoritative
   basic-strategy chart for your exact ruleset. The hint never replaces good
   judgment on the knife-edge cells — but for the vast majority of hands it is
   exactly right.

   You can turn hints (and the explanations, and the deviation note) off in the
   config: teaching.showBasicStrategyHints, explainHints, warnOnStrategyDeviation.


==============================================================================
  7.  CARD COUNTING (HI-LO)
==============================================================================

As cards appear, the readout under the top bar tracks the Hi-Lo count:

      HI-LO    Running +2     True +0.6     5.4 decks

WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN
   RUNNING count .. A running tally of the cards you've seen. Each card has a tag:

                       2  3  4  5  6   ->  +1   (low cards: good for the dealer
                                                 when gone, so seeing them is
                                                 good for YOU)
                       7  8  9         ->   0   (neutral)
                       10 J Q K A      ->  -1   (high cards / Aces)

                    You add the tags up as cards come out. A positive running
                    count means lots of low cards have already been played, so
                    the shoe is now rich in high cards — which favors the player.

   TRUE count ..... The running count divided by the number of DECKS REMAINING:

                       True = Running ÷ decks remaining

                    This matters because a running count of +5 is a big edge with
                    one deck left but small with six decks left. The true count
                    normalizes for that.

   decks remaining  How many decks' worth of cards are still in the shoe.

COLOR
   The values turn GREEN when the count favors the player and RED when it favors
   the house, so you can read the situation at a glance.

"COUNT AS YOU SEE THEM"
   The game counts each card the instant you'd actually see it at a real table —
   including the dealer's hidden card ONLY once it's turned face-up, never while
   it's face-down. The count carries across hands and RESETS to zero whenever the
   shoe is reshuffled (watch for the "Shuffling…" cue).

WHAT TO DO WITH IT
   Card counters bet MORE when the true count is high (the deck favors them) and
   less when it's low. The betting simulator (section 8) does not use the count;
   it's there to study betting systems. Counting here is for your own practice and
   awareness.

   This readout only appears for a normal depleting shoe. Only the Hi-Lo system is
   built in. Toggle it with teaching.showCardCounting and showTrueCount.


==============================================================================
  8.  THE BETTING SIMULATOR
==============================================================================

Tap 📊 in the top bar to open the simulator, then ← to return to the table. The
simulator answers a question: "If I used a betting system across many sessions,
how would I actually do?"

THE CONTROLS
   System ...... Which betting system to test:
                   * Martingale -- double your bet after every loss, drop back to
                     the minimum after a win (and hold it after a push). Once a
                     doubled bet would exceed the table maximum, it's capped
                     there. The idea is that one win recovers all prior losses.
                   * Flat -- bet the same amount every hand. This is the control
                     to compare against.
   Sessions .... How many separate sessions to simulate (1,000 / 2,000 / 5,000).
   Hands each .. How many hands each session plays (50 / 100 / 200).
   Run simulation  Starts the run; you'll briefly see "Running … sessions…".

HOW IT WORKS
   Each simulated session starts from the same bankroll and plays the chosen
   number of hands using PERFECT basic strategy for the actual play decisions; the
   betting system only decides HOW MUCH to wager each hand. Sessions end early if
   they go broke ("ruin"). It runs through the real game engine, so the results
   reflect this game's exact rules. Each run is a fresh random sample, so the
   numbers shift a little from run to run.

READING THE RESULTS
   You get five summary numbers:
      Mean ...... average profit/loss per session
      Median .... the middle session (half did better, half worse)
      Ruined .... percent of sessions that lost the entire bankroll
      Best ...... the luckiest session's result
      Worst ..... the worst session's result (often the full bankroll lost)

   Below them is a HISTOGRAM — a bar chart of final profit/loss across all
   sessions. Each bar is a money range; its height is how many sessions landed
   there. Bars LEFT of the dashed "$0" line are losing sessions (red); bars right
   are winning sessions (green).

THE LESSON MARTINGALE TEACHES
   Run Martingale and you'll typically see something striking: MOST sessions win
   a little (a positive median, a tall cluster of small wins just right of $0) —
   which is why the system feels like it works. But a large slice of sessions
   (often a third or so) BUST THE ENTIRE BANKROLL: a tall red spike at the far
   left (e.g. -$500). Those rare catastrophes outweigh the many small wins, so the
   MEAN still loses — and loses MORE than simply betting flat. Switch the system
   to Flat and the chart becomes a tight, roughly symmetric hump around a small
   loss, with no ruin spike. That contrast is the whole point: no betting pattern
   beats the house edge; it only reshapes how you lose.


==============================================================================
  9.  THE RULES THIS GAME USES
==============================================================================

These are the default rules (they can be changed — see section 10). The top-bar
shorthand "6D · S17 · 3:2" summarizes the first three.

SHOE
   * Six decks, shuffled together.
   * About 75% of the shoe is dealt before it reshuffles (the "penetration").

DEALER
   * Stands on all 17s, including soft 17 ("S17"). (The other common rule, where
     the dealer hits soft 17, is written "H17".)
   * Peeks for blackjack: if the up-card is an Ace or a ten, the dealer checks the
     hidden card before you play. On a dealer blackjack the round ends at once and
     you lose only your original bet (US style).

PAYOUTS
   * Blackjack pays 3:2 (a $10 blackjack wins $15). The stingier "6:5" is a config
     option; 3:2 is much better for the player.
   * Insurance pays 2:1.   * A normal win pays 1:1 (even money).

PLAYER OPTIONS
   * Double on any first two cards.
   * Double after split (DAS): allowed.
   * Split pairs up to four hands total.
   * Re-split Aces: not allowed. Split Aces get one card each.
   * Late surrender: allowed.
   * Insurance: offered when the dealer shows an Ace.

TABLE
   * Minimum bet $5, maximum bet $500.
   * Starting bankroll $500.
   * Chip denominations: $5, $25, $100, $500.

These defaults make a solid, common Las Vegas-style game (6 decks, S17, 3:2, DAS,
late surrender), which is quite player-friendly as blackjack goes.


==============================================================================
  10.  CHANGING THE RULES
==============================================================================

Every rule lives in ONE file:

      config/blackjack-rules.config.js

Edit a value there and the game plays differently — you never touch the game
logic. In development (npm run dev) changes apply instantly; for a built or
installed app, rebuild with "npm run build" so the new rules are bundled.

COMMON KNOBS
   shoe.numberOfDecks ......... 1, 2, 4, 6, or 8
   dealer.hitsSoft17 .......... false = S17 (default), true = H17
   payouts.blackjack .......... 1.5 = 3:2 (default), 1.2 = 6:5
   player.doubleRestriction ... 'any' (default), '9-11', or '10-11'
   player.doubleAfterSplit .... true/false
   player.surrender ........... 'none', 'late' (default), or 'early'
   player.maxSplitHands ....... 2, 3, or 4
   table.minBet / maxBet ...... table limits (maxBet also caps Martingale)
   table.startingBankroll ..... starting chips
   teaching.* ................. turn the hint, explanations, counting readout,
                                true count, and deviation warnings on or off

THE CAPABILITY GUARD
   A few options describe rules this version doesn't fully implement yet. Rather
   than play them WRONG (the worst kind of bug in a teaching app), the game shows
   a "Config notes" warning on screen and in the console, and falls back to safe,
   documented behavior. These currently include:

      * shoe.continuousShuffle  -> falls back to a normal reshuffle; the count
                                   readout is hidden.
      * dealer.peeksForBlackjack: false (European no-peek) -> the dealer peeks
                                   (US style) instead.
      * player.surrender: 'early' -> treated as late surrender.
      * player.resplitAces / hitSplitAces -> split Aces still get one card each.
      * teaching.countingSystem other than 'hi-lo' -> the readout uses Hi-Lo.

   If you see a config note, that's why.


==============================================================================
  11.  SOUND & MOTION
==============================================================================

   * Sound effects (card flicks, chips, shuffle, win/lose/push tones) are
     generated on the fly — there are no audio files, and they work fully offline.
     Tap 🔊 / 🔇 to mute or un-mute; the setting is remembered.
   * On iPhones, sound only starts after your first tap (an Apple requirement),
     so the very first action may be silent — that's normal.
   * Card and chip animations respect your phone's "Reduce Motion" accessibility
     setting; turn that on at the OS level for a still, instant interface.


==============================================================================
  12.  SAVING, RESETTING & RUNNING OUT OF CHIPS
==============================================================================

SAVING
   Your bankroll and the mute setting are saved on your device automatically. The
   bankroll is only saved at safe moments (between hands), so if you close the app
   mid-hand, that unfinished hand is voided — not charged — when you return.

RESETTING
   Tap ↺ in the top bar (between hands) to reset your bankroll to the starting
   amount and begin a fresh session.

RUNNING OUT
   If your bankroll drops below the table minimum, the game shows "Out of chips."
   with a RE-BUY button to restock to the starting bankroll. (It's only chips —
   re-buy freely.)


==============================================================================
  13.  GLOSSARY
==============================================================================

   Bankroll ......... The chips you have to bet with.
   Bust ............. A hand total over 21 — an automatic loss.
   Push ............. A tie with the dealer; your bet is returned.
   Natural / Blackjack  An Ace + a 10-value card on the first two cards = 21.
   Soft hand ........ A hand with an Ace counted as 11 (e.g. soft 17 = A+6).
   Hard hand ........ No Ace, or an Ace forced to count as 1.
   Up-card .......... The dealer's face-up card.
   Hole card ........ The dealer's face-down card.
   Peek ............. The dealer checking the hole card for blackjack before play.
   Double (down) .... Double the bet for exactly one more card.
   Split ............ Turn a pair into two separate hands.
   DAS .............. "Double After Split" — doubling a hand created by splitting.
   Surrender ........ Forfeit half the bet to fold a bad hand.
   Insurance ........ A side bet that the dealer has blackjack (offered vs an Ace).
   Penetration ...... How deep into the shoe the dealer deals before reshuffling.
   Shoe ............. The combined stack of decks the cards are dealt from.
   Running count .... The Hi-Lo tally of cards seen so far.
   True count ....... Running count divided by decks remaining.
   EV ............... "Expected value" — average result of a play, in bets.
   Ruin ............. Losing your entire bankroll.
   House edge ....... The casino's built-in long-run advantage.
